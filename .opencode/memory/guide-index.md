# Guide Index

Use this file to decide where public-guide content belongs inside `website/content/guide/`. The root guide is "A guide to becoming a software architect." Canonical detail lives in the guide `_index.md` files and page front matter.

This index belongs to `website/`. Content paths below
are relative to the workspace root.

## Top-Level Sections

- `website/content/guide/introduction/` - guide landing pages, audience framing, and navigation helpers.
- `website/content/guide/architecture/` - the core architecture body of knowledge.
- `website/content/guide/organization/` - team, product, value-stream, business, and alignment context.
- `website/content/guide/role/` - architect responsibilities, role variants, collaboration, and career growth.
- `website/content/guide/competencies/` - hard and soft skill sections such as modeling, communication, and requirements.
- `website/content/guide/specializations/` - specialization tracks such as foundation, application, data, and platform.
- `website/content/guide/library.md` - consolidated recommended reading across the guide.

## Architecture Section

- `website/content/guide/architecture/fundamentals/` - core pages covering what architecture is, architecture characteristics, styles, visualization, process, and sample architecture.
- `website/content/guide/architecture/characteristics/` - pages on identifying, measuring, governing, and improving quality attributes such as availability, interoperability, modifiability, performance, security, testability, usability, and scalability.
- `website/content/guide/architecture/styles/` - style-selection pages plus concrete style pages for layered monoliths, modular monoliths, pipeline monoliths, microkernel monoliths, service-based systems, event-driven systems, space-based systems, orchestration-driven systems, and microservices.
- `website/content/guide/architecture/domains/` - DDD-focused pages covering business domains, domain modeling, building blocks, bounded contexts, context integration, discovery, evolution, best practices, and applying DDD.
- `website/content/guide/architecture/boundaries/` - coupling, modularity, components, services, sharing, operational data, analytical data, distributed workflows, contracts, and trade-off analysis.
- `website/content/guide/architecture/documentation/` - architecture documentation, diagrams, models, descriptions, ADRs, and documentation management.
- `website/content/guide/architecture/validation/` - architecture alignment, metrics, evaluation, risk, economics, governance, and implementation/testing fit.

## Organization, Role, Competencies, And Specializations

- `website/content/guide/organization/` - pages such as `team.md`, `value-streams.md`, `engineering.md`, `product.md`, `company.md`, `alignment.md`, and `goals.md`.
- `website/content/guide/role/` - pages such as `role-overview.md`, `classification.md`, `collaboration.md`, and `career.md`.
- `website/content/guide/competencies/modeling/` - modeling overview plus framework and notation pages such as C4, viewpoints and perspectives, 4+1, TOGAF, flowchart, Event Storming, UML, and ArchiMate.
- `website/content/guide/competencies/communication/` - communication pages for visual, written, spoken, effective, documentation, remote, and multi-cultural work.
- `website/content/guide/competencies/requirements/` - requirement definition, collection, business use cases, scenarios, selection, writing, functional requirements, non-functional requirements, testing, and communication pages.
- `website/content/guide/competencies/facilitation/` - facilitation overview.
- `website/content/guide/competencies/leadership/` - leadership overview.
- `website/content/guide/competencies/management/` - management overview.
- `website/content/guide/specializations/foundation/` - foundational software and architecture concepts.
- `website/content/guide/specializations/applications/` - web, backend, transactional, and messaging systems.
- `website/content/guide/specializations/data/` - warehousing, analytics, AI/ML, and business intelligence.
- `website/content/guide/specializations/platform/` - hosting, security, and DevOps.

Before adding guide pages, check the nearest section folder, its `_index.md`, and neighboring pages in `website/content/guide/` for exact titles, descriptions, and paths.
